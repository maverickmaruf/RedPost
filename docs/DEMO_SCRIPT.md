# Demo script

**Filming rule:** no one under 18 on camera, in voice or by name. Film an adult's hands on a phone, or record the screen. Say dataset names out loud.

## 30-second video

| Time | Picture | Words (voice-over, adult) |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 s | Title screen, tap the planet 5 times | "Red Outpost. Keep four astronauts alive on Mars for 30 sols." |
| 5-12 s | Sol 12, forecast card: "Big solar storm reaches Mars on sol 13. This message travelled 16 minutes from Earth." | "This storm warning is a real NASA DONKI forecast from May 2024, and it really took 16 minutes to reach Mars." |
| 12-20 s | Tap Shelter, End sol, report: storm hit, small dose | "Shelter blocks the radiation, but no science today and the greenhouse is half-tended. Every choice costs something." |
| 20-27 s | Review chart, you vs dashed Flight Director | "Fail, and the Flight Director shows you what happened, and a better plan." |
| 27-30 s | Data drawer badges | "Real NASA data. Works offline. English and Bangla." |

## 240-second video

1. **The problem (0-25 s).** Space lessons for kids are posters and facts. Real missions are trade-offs. The 2026 challenge: let kids run an outpost and feel those trade-offs.
2. **One sol (25-70 s).** The meters: power, air, food, radiation. Sun today from the Mars season (NASA GISS Mars24) and site latitude. Heaters follow InSight's measured temperatures. Set the air plant, greenhouse, radio and crew, then End sol.
3. **The real storms (70-120 s).** Name the data: NASA DONKI WSA-ENLIL, the CCMC model that forecasts CME arrivals at Mars. Show the May 2024 month: 22 forecasts. The warning only reaches you if the radio is on, after the real Earth-Mars delay from JPL's planetary positions.
4. **Fail and learn (120-165 s).** Ignore the warnings, walk outside, hit the radiation limit. Flight Director's Review: why, the ghost chart, and a cited fact (Curiosity RAD measured 8,100 µGy in the May 2024 storm).
5. **Choose a site (165-195 s).** MOLA laser-altimeter slope map. Tap Gale's mountain flank: "too steep". Gusev in June 2025 gets too little sun (southern winter), which is what happened to the Spirit rover.
6. **Trust (195-225 s).** The ⓘ Data drawer: dataset IDs, URLs, retrieval dates, badges for cache, simulated and archived data. The About screen: which numbers are real NASA values and which are simplified.
7. **Reach (225-240 s).** Free, offline, cheap phones, Bangla and English, open source.
